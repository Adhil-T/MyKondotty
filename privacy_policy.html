Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 02-05-2025

At MyKondotty, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please refrain from using our services.

We collect both personal and non-personal information to enhance your experience and improve our offerings. Personal information includes details you voluntarily provide such as your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses, payment information, and account credentials. Non-personal information is collected automatically and includes data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and user activity (like pages visited or links clicked). We also use cookies and similar technologies to understand how users interact with our site and to support marketing efforts.

The information we collect is used to operate, improve, and personalize our services. Specifically, we use it to process transactions, provide customer support, send updates and promotional messages (where consent is given), detect and prevent fraud, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. Your data helps us tailor your experience, respond to your inquiries, and deliver content that matches your interests.

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share it with trusted service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as payment processing, website hosting, data analysis, and customer service. We may also share data with affiliates or partners, but only with your permission or as part of a co-branded service. In certain circumstances, we may disclose information if required by law or if necessary to protect our legal rights or respond to legal processes. In the event of a business merger, sale, or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction.

Depending on your location, you may have specific privacy rights. These may include the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, ask for deletion of your data, restrict or object to certain processing, or receive your information in a portable format. If you provided consent for us to process your data, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. To exercise these rights, you can contact us at hellomykondotty@gmail.com.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. This includes complying with legal requirements, resolving disputes, and enforcing our agreements. Once the data is no longer needed, we take steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

To protect your information, we use a variety of technical, administrative, and physical security measures. While we strive to ensure the safety of your data, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you are accessing our services from a country outside the location of our servers, please note that your data may be transferred and stored across international borders. If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your information, please contact us at hellomykondotty@gmail.com or by mail at MyKondotty. We appreciate your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy at every step.
