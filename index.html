About My Kondotty

Connecting Our Local Community

My Kondotty is a hyperlocal digital assistant built for the people and businesses of Kondotty. Our mission is to connect the community through an easy-to-use chatbot that offers everything from local shopping and digital marketing to job listings and emergency contacts—all in one place.

Whether you're a resident, a small business owner, or a visitor, My Kondotty helps you stay informed, connected, and empowered. Discover local shops, promote your services, find events, and get real-time updates about what's happening around you. We're here to make local life smarter and more convenient.

Who We Are

We’re a local-first platform dedicated to supporting the Kondotty community by offering digital tools that simplify everyday tasks—shopping, services, communication, and more.

Our Philosophy

Empowering people with information and access is the first step toward a connected society. We believe technology should serve communities, not isolate them.

How We Work

Our chatbot collects, organizes, and delivers verified local information through an interactive, user-friendly interface—available anytime on your phone or device.
